Built like a banyan

For centuries, Indian merchants did business under one tree. Its canopy took the sun. Its hanging roots grew into new pillars as it spread. It outlived every trader who sat beneath it, and European travellers named it after them: the banyan.

Our story

The banyan was this country's first cool commercial building. It is still the best brief for one: a canopy that takes the heat, a structure that renews itself, and a life measured in generations.

Murarilal Kumawat has worked on India's buildings since 1991: thirty-five years of stone, tile and waterproofing on more than 55 projects, from hospitals and data centres to schools and offices. In those years buildings grew taller and glassier, and cities grew hotter.

Murman Engineering was founded in 2025 to build commercial buildings the banyan's way: cool by design, renewed instead of replaced, and made to last.

What we promise

Cool

The canopy takes the sun, so everything under it stays cooler. In our work: roof cooling, shading and the building envelope.

Renewed, not replaced

Hanging roots grow into new pillars. The tree strengthens and extends itself and never has to start again. In our work: retrofit, circular fit-out and low-carbon materials.

Made to last

One tree stands for centuries. In our work: whole-life design, clean construction, and performance checked in use.
