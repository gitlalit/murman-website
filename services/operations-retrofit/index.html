Operational performance and retrofit

For buildings in use. Renovating emits far less than rebuilding. We find where the energy goes, cool the roof, and upgrade lighting and cooling with a partner who can be repaid from the savings.

Who it is for

Owners and facility heads of existing offices, hospitals, hotels, schools and warehouses.

What we do

  • An energy audit with our energy service partner: where the power goes, hour by hour
  • Roof cooling with new waterproofing, by our own teams
  • Lighting and controls: LED, sensors and timers
  • Cooling plant: service and tune first, replace old plant later, under a performance contract
  • Measurement before and after, reported and open to a third party to verify

What you get

  • An audit report
  • A retrofit plan with the cost and payback of each step
  • Before and after measurements
  • A yearly review of energy and water use

How it is priced

The audit and the roof are quoted on scope. Plant upgrades can be paid for by the energy service partner and repaid from measured savings.

Questions we are asked

What have retrofits returned elsewhere?

Two measured Mumbai cases documented by NRDC and ASCI: Mahindra Towers spent Rs 18.9 lakh, cut power use by 14% and recovered the cost in under six months. Godrej Bhavan spent Rs 53.8 lakh, saved 12.3% and recovered it in 4.7 years.

Do tenants have to move out?

No. Roof work happens above them, lighting is changed floor by floor, and plant work is scheduled around operating hours.

Do you do the mechanical and electrical work yourselves?

No. Plant upgrades are done by our energy service partner. Roof, waterproofing and finishing work is done by our own teams. You have one point of contact for all of it.
