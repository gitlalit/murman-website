Digital MRV: AI handles the evidence, engineers make the decisions

Measurement, reporting and verification is the backbone of credible climate action. Most of it is documents, photographs and checklists. AI handles that volume, and a qualified engineer reviews and signs every output.

  1. Reads

    Regulations, drawings, area statements and bills of quantities.Approval and compliance files start in hours, not weeks.

  2. Watches

    Site photographs from your team's phones.Quality and compliance issues are flagged the same day.

  3. Writes

    Compliance reports, quality records and lender evidence packs.Your team stays focused on delivery.

  4. Engineer

    Reviews, decides and signs.Backed by 35 years of civil and finishing work.
