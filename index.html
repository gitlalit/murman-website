AI does the paperwork. Engineers make the calls.

Construction runs on documents, photos and checklists. Most of that work is slow and repetitive, which is what AI does well. An engineer checks and signs every output before it reaches you.

  1. Reads

    Rules, drawings, area statements and BOQs. The FSI working and the clearance file start in hours, not weeks.

  2. Watches

    Site photos from your team's phones. Quality issues are flagged the same day, floor by floor.

  3. Writes

    Compliance reports, quality reports, lender packs. Your team spends its time on site, not on formats.

  4. Engineer

    Reviews, decides and signs. 35 years of finishing and civil work behind every judgement call.
