How the incentive works

Linked to the rating

The additional FSI depends on the green building rating your project is pre-certified for: 3% for silver, 5% for gold and 7% for platinum, applied to the basic built-up area.

Earned at completion

The committed rating has to be achieved when the building is complete. Murman YIELD tracks the evidence through to the occupancy certificate so the incentive is protected.

Checked plot by plot

Eligibility and the exact figure depend on the planning authority, zoning and the plot itself. A free assessment confirms what applies to your project.
