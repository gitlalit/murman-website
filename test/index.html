What the banyan teaches a building

Three ideas run through everything we do. Together they describe what UN bodies call a near-zero emission and resilient building.

Cool

The canopy takes the sun, so everything under it stays cooler. In our work: roof cooling, shading and the building envelope.

Renewed, not replaced

Hanging roots grow into new pillars. The tree strengthens and extends itself and never has to start again. In our work: retrofit, circular fit-out and low-carbon materials.

Made to last

One tree stands for centuries. In our work: whole-life design, clean construction, and performance checked in use.

What the owner gets back

A green building costs a little more to build, and gives more back over its life.

Lower bills

Green-rated buildings use 20 to 30% less energy and 30 to 50% less water than a conventional building of the same size.

IGBC, Green New Buildings

More floor area

In UDCPR areas such as Navi Mumbai, Thane, Kalyan-Dombivli and Pune, a green rating adds 3, 5 or 7% to the basic FSI. Greater Mumbai has no green FSI.
