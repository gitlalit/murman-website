How we work with partners

A banyan grows by adding pillars. We do the same. Our own teams execute finishing and roof work. For the rest we bring one accountable partner for each task, and we stay responsible for the result.

  • Certification: accredited professionals and an independent certifier (IGBC, GRIHA, EDGE or LEED)
  • Energy audits and plant upgrades: an energy service company that can be repaid from the savings
  • Environmental clearance studies: an accredited consultant
  • Materials: suppliers who provide test reports and product declarations

What we do not offer

We work on commercial buildings only. We do not prepare ESG reports, file RERA returns or sell carbon credits. When a client needs those, we introduce a specialist.
