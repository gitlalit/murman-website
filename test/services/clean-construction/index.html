Clean construction and environmental compliance

For sites under construction. Dust, debris and clearance conditions are now enforced on site. We check them every month, so the site keeps running.

Who it is for

Developers, project managers and contractors with a live site in the Mumbai region or Pune.

What we do

  • A site check against air-quality and dust conditions set by the municipal corporation
  • A debris plan: sorting on site, authorised transporters and recyclers, and receipts kept
  • A register of environmental clearance conditions, with who is responsible for each
  • A monthly review on site, with photographs and a fix list
  • A short session for site staff on what inspectors look for

What you get

  • A site check report with a fix list
  • A compliance register
  • Monthly review notes
  • A file of waste receipts and monitoring records, ready for inspection

How it is priced

The site check is a defined job. Monthly reviews are a retainer that depends on the size of the site. All of it is quoted on scope.

How it runs

  1. Site visit

    One visit against the conditions that apply to your site.

  2. Fix list

    What to correct this week, this month and before the next inspection.

  3. Register

    Every condition listed with an owner and a due date.

  4. Monthly review

    A site walk, records checked, register updated.

  5. Close-out

    A complete file at completion.

Questions we are asked

Why does this matter now?

Mumbai issued 1,206 stop-work notices for dust by February 2026, and a circular of September 2026 asks the developer and the architect to sign an undertaking. A stopped site costs far more than a monthly check.

Which rules apply to my site?

It depends on size and location: municipal air-quality conditions, the 2025 construction and demolition waste rules for projects of 20,000 sq m and above, and any environmental clearance conditions. The site check lists them for you.

Do you prepare clearance applications?

Clearance studies are prepared by an accredited consultant whom we bring in. We keep the site in line with the conditions once they are issued.
