Low-carbon, circular fit-out and finishing

For new interiors and refurbishments. Stone, tile, waterproofing and interior works, specified and executed with low-carbon, low-emission materials, with fit-out waste kept in use.

Who it is for

Owners, occupiers and project managers fitting out a floor or a whole building, including global firms setting up offices in India.

What we do

  • A material schedule with low-carbon and recycled-content options, each with its test report or product declaration
  • Execution of stone, tiling, waterproofing and allied finishing by our own teams
  • Low-emission adhesives, paints and sealants, for healthier indoor air
  • Fit-out waste sorted on site and sent to recyclers: the circular economy in practice
  • Records kept in the form interior ratings ask for, such as IGBC Green Interiors and LEED for interiors

What you get

  • Finished floors, walls and wet areas
  • A record of materials used and waste recycled
  • An indoor air checklist at handover
  • A handover file with warranties and maintenance notes

How it is priced

Executed work is priced by BOQ rates. Advisory work is quoted on scope.

How it runs

  1. Brief and BOQ review

    We read the drawings and the BOQ and mark where carbon and cost can come down.

  2. Material schedule

    Alternatives are listed with prices, lead times and declarations.

  3. Mock-up and approval

    One bay or one room is built for sign-off.

  4. Execution

    Work proceeds floor by floor, with waste sorted as it is produced.

  5. Handover

    Snag list closed, records handed over.

Questions we are asked

Does a low-carbon fit-out cost more?

Savills reports that circular fit-outs cut embodied carbon by 25 to 55%, with a premium of 10 to 15% that is recovered in 5 to 10 years. On many items, such as blended-cement screeds, there is no premium at all.

Can you work in an occupied building?

Yes. Work is phased floor by floor, and noisy or dusty work is scheduled outside office hours.

What is the team's record?

Finishing work on 55+ projects since 1991 through Lalit Enterprises: marble and stone, tiling and, on some projects, waterproofing.
