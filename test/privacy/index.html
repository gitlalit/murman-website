What this website collects, in plain words. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Visits

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see which pages are read. Google sets cookies in your browser to do this. We use the figures to improve the site, and for nothing else.

The form prepares a message from the details you type, and you choose to send it to us on WhatsApp. When email delivery is switched on, the form will send your details to our mailbox through a form service. We use them only to reply to your request.
