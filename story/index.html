SERVICE 3 OF 5

Low-carbon materials and circularity

For structures and envelopes. Embodied carbon is decided in the mix design and the BOQ, long before the building uses a unit of power.

  • A whole-life carbon baseline: embodied plus operational
  • Concrete with GGBS or fly ash, AAC blocks and other low-carbon walling
  • A BOQ with a priced lower-carbon alternative for every major item

On the model: the glass removed to show the structure, a block wall going up, and material stock on site.

SERVICE 4 OF 5

Clean construction and environmental compliance

For sites under construction. A stopped site costs more than a clean one.

  • A site check against the dust and air-quality conditions of the municipal corporation
  • A debris plan with authorised transporters and recyclers, and receipts kept
  • A monthly review on site with photographs and a fix list

On the model: dust screens, scaffold netting, a wheel wash at the gate and covered material.

SERVICE 5 OF 5

Operational performance and retrofit

For buildings in use. Renovating emits far less than rebuilding, and the roof is the place to start.

  • An energy audit: where the power goes, hour by hour
  • Roof cooling with new waterproofing, by our own teams
  • Lighting, controls and cooling plant, measured before and after

On the model: keep scrolling. The roof is coated, solar and fins arrive, blinds go up and trees grow.

Drag the slider and watch the building change. Orange arrows show heat coming in, blue arrows show it reflected away.

Shade first

External fins stop the sun before it reaches the glass. Heat stopped outside never reaches the air conditioner.

Then the glass and the roof

Solar control film or low-e glass, and a reflective roof. One maker reports 62% of solar heat rejected by a retrofit film.

Then the plant

Tune and right-size the cooling. Mahindra Towers cut power by 14% and Godrej Bhavan by 12.3%.

Illustration, not a completed project. Sources: Solar Gard reference case, 2023; NRDC and ASCI retrofit cases.
